Hi, my name is Gal, and I believe that design is all about people.

For as long as I can remember, I’ve been curious about people – how we interact, what motivates us, and what shapes our behavior. This curiosity led me to complete a B.A. in Psychology and Sociology, which continues to shape the way I approach design.

With my background in psychology, I don’t just design for users – I create for people. I believe good design is about understanding needs, solving problems, and turning complex challenges into simple, meaningful experiences. I’m an analytical thinker, a collaborative team player, and a lifelong learner, with an eye for aesthetics and attention to detail.

In my free time, you’ll find me listening to music, exploring photography, dancing, or watching a good documentary. I’m also a bit of a Harry Potter nerd, surprisingly good at finding super-simple recipes, and capable of typing over 100 words per minute. And, of course, I’m happiest when I’m with Bugatti, my dog and favorite company in the world.
