Silver Spoon

Silver Spoon is a silver colored, black sesame and chocolate flavored ice cream.
It has a bittersweet taste, and each flavor combines black sesame with a different type of high quality chocolate.

It is a sophisticated, unique, and high-end ice cream brand.

This project was created during my studies.
