Camilo

Camilo is a smart diffuser based on essential oils, that contains five different scents. The scent changes according to time and situation: an awakening scent in the morning, a focussing scent during work, an energizing scent in the afternoon, a relaxing scent in the evening, and a scent that facilitates sleep at night.

It is a boutique, original and innovative product, that improves lifestyle, wellbeing and daily routine.

This project was created during my studies.
