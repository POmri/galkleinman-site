We Love Easy Recipes

Recipez is a recipe app that offers easy-to-make recipes based on ingredients the users currently have.

The users can manage their food preferences, make a personal pantry list, and explore recipes that match their eating habits.

Running out of time and ingredients?

The need: Cooking takes time and effort. Many people don’t have the time or energy to plan their meals ahead, and when looking for recipes online they find out that they don’t have all the ingredients required.

Saving time, making cooking easy

Our solution: A mobile recipe app that offers quick, simple, and diverse recipes using ingredients the users have in their homes. The app learns the users’ food preferences and common ingredients and provides the perfect recipes for them.

Helping you cook smarter, not harder

Our main goals:

  1. Save users time and energy.
  2. Create a positive experience by offering personalized recipes and using clear, visual cooking instructions.

Perfect for busy days and low-energy moments

Our users fit into two main types:

  1. Lack of time: Busy people who have little time to spend on planing and preparing their meals.
  2. Lack of energy: People who want to spend minimal effort preapring food.

From research to wireframes

Where design meets positive user experience

The main goal of the design process was to create an app that delivers a positive experience, and aligns with the application’s goals and values.

Learn how it works and start your journey

Your next meal, just a search away

Add ingredients to find recipe suggestions according to your preferred meal.
Choose from popular ingredients or directly from your virtual pantry list.

Choose your recipe, with personalized filters

Pick your preferred recipe or add filters according to your personal preferences to find the perfect recipe.

The app will offer either a perfect match, where all ingredients are available, or a partial match, where some ingredients are missing but the recipe can still be made.

Everything you need for your next quick meal

Everything you need for your perfect meal in one place, to get the best cooking experience.
The recipe screen includes all the information you need to know about the recipe.
The information is organized into three categories:
Ingredients, instructions, and nutrition info.

Under the ingredients section, you can choose the serving size and all of the amounts of the ingredients will automatically update. If there are any missing ingredients, use the swap button to find a suitable replacement.

The recipe instructions combines visual infographics and detailed written instructions, so you can take a quick look or read the full directions, step by step.

Discover new recipes tailored to you

Use the favorites screen to quickly find all your beloved recipes.

In the discover screen you can explore new recipes and find recommendations tailored to your taste.

Create your pantry, customize your preferences

Build your personal pantry list of common ingredients you frequently use.

Manage your food preferences and eating habits, to get personalized recipes suited for you.
